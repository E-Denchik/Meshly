Initial development build.

* Local account creation with optional Jami username registration
* Contact requests, accept/reject, block/unblock
* 1:1 text chat with local history, delivery/read receipts, file and image attachments
* 1:1 audio and video calls with camera switch, mute, and an over-lock-screen incoming call UI
* Password-protected account export/import
* Configurable OpenDHT bootstrap nodes, UPnP toggle
* Local diagnostic log export
* Notifications for incoming calls and messages without Firebase Cloud Messaging

Runs on a mock engine pending the real libjami native integration (see PHASE2_BUILD.md).
